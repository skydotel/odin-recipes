Ingredients

  • Buns
  • Beef
  • Cheese
  • tomatoes

Instructions

  1. Cook the beef on both sides untill medium
  2. Put the beef on half of the bun
  3. Add cheese on top of the beef
  4. Add tomatoes on top of the cheese
  5. Add ketchup on top of the tomatoes
  6. Finally, place other half of the bun on top of the burger
