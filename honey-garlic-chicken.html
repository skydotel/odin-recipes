Ingredients

  • 4 Chicken Thighs
  • Honey
  • Garlic
  • Rice
  • Garnishments

Instructions

  1. Cook the chicken thighs at 350 degrees for 30 to 40 minutes in an oven
  2. Mix the honey and garlic together with water to make the sauce
  3. Pour the sauce over the chicken thighs
  4. Place the sauced chicken thighs on a plate of white rice
  5. Add garnishment to the top of the sauced chicken thighs
