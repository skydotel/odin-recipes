Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 cup chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. In a bowl, mix together flour, baking soda, and salt.
  3. In another bowl, cream together butter and sugar until smooth.
  4. Beat in eggs one at a time, then stir in vanilla extract.
  5. Gradually blend in the dry ingredients.
  6. Stir in chocolate chips.
  7. Drop by rounded spoonfuls onto ungreased cookie sheets.
  8. Bake for 10-12 minutes or until edges are golden brown.
