Ingredients

  • 1 cup sugar
  • 2 cups flour
  • 3 eggs

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mix sugar and eggs in a bowl.
  3. Add flour and mix until smooth.
  4. Pour the batter into a greased baking pan.
  5. Bake for 30 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
